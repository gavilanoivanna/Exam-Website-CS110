In Game Roles

  • Vanguard: Frontline protectors with high health pools who absorb damage, create space, and shield teammates. (Emma Frost, Thor, Doctor Strange)
  • Duelist: High damage attackers focused on flanking, poking from a distance, and securing quick eliminations. (Spiderman, Namor, Hela)
  • Strategist: Support specialists responsible for healing allies, boosting damage, and providing tactical battlefield utility. (Luna Snow, Invisible Woman, Jubilee)

Steps to Win a Match

  1. Build a balanced team: Most teams follow a composition of 2 vanguards, 2 duelists, and 2 strategists.
  2. Winning 1v1s: Some duelists (such as Spider-Man) can dive into the backlines where typically strategists are located, allowing them to get a pick and win the fight so it becomes a 6v5.
  3. Protect strategists: The enemy team will focus on your team's strategists, so Vanguards should peel (fall back) and protect them so enough healing can be done.
  4. Secure objective: Depending on whether it's escorting the payload or fighting for the domination point, that's the main focus of the game that'll lead you to win.
